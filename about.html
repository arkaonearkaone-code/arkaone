ABOUT ARKA ELITE PROPERTIES

Your Trusted Partner in Real Estate. Your Gateway to Valuable Investments. ARKA ELITE PROPERTIES is a Hyderabad-based real estate marketing and distribution company committed to transforming the way people discover, evaluate and invest in real estate.
With a strong foundation in sales, business development, operations and customer relationship management, the company has steadily expanded its presence across Hyderabad and Visakhapatnam, specializing in premium open plots, villa plots and thoughtfully planned gated community developments.
Our journey has been strengthened through strategic industry associations, including our initial association with the prestigious VASAVI Group as an exclusive marketing partner. We have further expanded our market presence through professional collaborations, customer engagement initiatives and participation in national and international real estate exhibitions.

Arka Elite Founder

Founded in 2019 by Mr. Alla Ramakrishna (RK Alla) and Mrs. Anjana V, ARKA ELITE PROPERTIES was established with a clear vision: to create a real estate ecosystem built on trust, transparency, professional excellence and enduring customer relationships.
Under the dynamic leadership of our Chairman & Managing Director, Mr. Alla Ramakrishna, and Executive Director & CEO, Mrs. Anjana V, ARKA ELITE PROPERTIES continues to pursue its mission of delivering professional, customer-centric real estate solutions.
